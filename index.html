The artist

Art born
from depth

Micson Upton is a poet and music artist whose work lives at the crossroads of spoken word and instrumental sound — a space where raw emotion meets deliberate craft.

Known for beats that breathe, instrumentals that ache, and verses that linger long after they've been heard, Micson creates music for those who feel deeply and say little.

His sound is intimate. His words, unflinching.

"I don't make music to be heard. I make it so the quiet has somewhere to go."

— Micson Upton
